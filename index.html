The Apology

To My Dear Sweetest Person Ever ❤️,

If you're reading this, thank you for giving me a few moments of your time 🫶.

We've been together through school, intermediate, and now engineering. When I look back at all these years, I realize how many memories we've created, how many conversations we've shared, and how many moments became special simply because you were there.

Over the last week, things changed.

I know I wasn't talking to you the way I used to. I became distant, and whether I realized it at the time or not, my actions made you feel ignored and unimportant.

I want you to know that hurting you was never my intention.

But intentions don't change the fact that my behavior affected you. What matters is that I made one of the most important people in my life feel neglected, and for that, I am truly sorry.

You have been there through so many phases of my life. Through school memories, college days, stressful times, happy moments, random conversations, and countless laughs.

Maybe I didn't show that properly this past week.

That was my mistake.

I miss talking to you.
I miss sharing random things with you.
I miss the comfort of knowing you're there.
Most importantly, I miss my best friend.

This website isn't meant to force you to forgive me. It isn't meant to make excuses. I just wanted to tell you something that I should have said earlier:

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for making you feel ignored.
I'm sorry for making you question your place in my life.
I'm sorry for the hurt my actions caused.

Thank you for all the years, all the memories, and for being the amazing person you've always been.

From,
this waste fellow 😑
